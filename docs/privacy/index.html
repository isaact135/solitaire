Solitaire Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Solitaire is a Klondike card game for iPhone. It doesn’t collect any information about you.

What the app collects

Nothing. Solitaire has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it contains no third-party code. It never connects to the internet, so nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

So you can pick up where you left off, the app saves your current game, its undo history and the draw mode you chose. This is stored only on your iPhone, and it leaves the device only in backups you control, such as iCloud Backup. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Solitaire doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
